04 / HOW I WORK

A repeatable process.
Clear accountability.

The same monthly operating cycle keeps targeting, implementation and reporting connected.

01

Target

Map each keyword to a target page, search volume and tracking status.

02

Track

Record monthly positions, ranking URLs and alternate pages to catch cannibalisation or the wrong page ranking.

03

Group & brief

Segment rankings, prioritise opportunities and write implementation-ready tasks with affected URLs and required changes.

04

Report

Review clicks, impressions, users and authority; separate brand and non-brand and explain what changed.
